Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-11-23

This document explains how Nightform handles information when you use the app.

Health and Sleep Data

Nightform requests access to Apple Health only after you choose whether to grant permission. Depending on the permissions you grant, the app reads sleep stages, heart rate, heart-rate variability, respiratory rate, blood oxygen and related metrics to present sleep and recovery reports. Nightform currently does not request write access to Apple Health. You can change these permissions in Apple Health settings.

Microphone and Audio

Microphone access is optional and is requested only for sound-recording features you choose to enable. Sound clips are stored locally and are not classified as snoring, speech or medical events. You can disable microphone access in iOS Settings. Exported recordings or reports are created only when you choose the corresponding action.

AI and Network Features

When you use an AI-powered feature, the prompt and feature parameters needed to fulfill your request may be sent to the relevant AI service provider. Do not enter sensitive personal information into AI prompts. AI features require a network connection and may be unavailable when the service is not configured or is temporarily unavailable.

Local Storage and Exports

Preferences, listening history, recommendations and subscription cache are stored on your device. CSV, PDF, images and other exports are created when you request them and are then handled by the destination you choose in the system share sheet. Nightform does not sell your health data or use it for advertising.

Subscriptions and Purchases

Purchases, subscriptions, trials, renewals, cancellations, refunds and restore-purchase requests are processed by Apple through the App Store. Nightform receives entitlement status needed to unlock features, not your full payment-card details.

Data Retention and Deletion

You can delete records from Apple Health through Apple's Health app. You can remove local Nightform data by deleting the app and its data from iOS Settings. Exported files must be deleted from the locations where you saved or shared them. Information processed by Apple or an AI provider is subject to that provider's retention policies.

Children

Nightform is not intended for children to use without appropriate permission from a parent or guardian and, where required, Ask to Buy.

Policy Updates and Contact

Material changes to this policy will be reflected in an updated app or policy document. For privacy questions or deletion requests, contact Nightform support at 948425230@qq.com.

Nightform provides general wellness information, not medical diagnosis or treatment.
