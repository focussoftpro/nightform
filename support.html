Purchases and Subscriptions

Use Restore Purchases on the Nightform subscription page to restore eligible purchases made with the same Apple Account. Subscription management, cancellation and refund requests are handled by Apple through your App Store account.

Health and Microphone Permissions

Health data access is controlled in Apple Health under your profile's Apps settings. Microphone access is controlled in iOS Settings. Nightform can only display health information that is available and permitted on your device.
